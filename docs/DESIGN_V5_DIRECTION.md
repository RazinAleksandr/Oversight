# Oversight v5 — atlas and dispatches

13 October 2026. User rejected build 4 and authorized an independent, drastic
screen-by-screen redesign, with proposals reviewed inside the agent team before
implementation. No additional user approval is required.

## Diagnosis and decision

The map was technically retained but buried behind World navigation. The flat
charcoal style made briefings, services and geopolitical state look interchangeable.
Small successful hit targets did not establish a convincing game identity.

The team converged on a **political atlas with official dispatches**. Restore spatial
context and institutions as the visual subject of the game. Operations have an ink
blue field; authored decisions have an ivory reading surface. National colors and
emblems represent actual entities. Crimson represents observed danger, and gold
marks available decisions. The geography is illustrative; overlays never invent threat locations or certainty.

## Composition contract

- Agenda: visible strategic atlas and national condition beside an official dispatch
  queue. The player can move directly from a country to its record. Commitments and
  incidents remain accessible from the board. The quarter action stays anchored.
- World: map is the default working surface. Select a country or open treaty work;
  country intelligence and diplomacy keep a clear return to the atlas.
- Briefings: authored source and substantial readable paper; explicit response
  review. Restore document identity without noisy textures or arbitrary stamps.
- Management: distinguish a national program ledger, institutional company records,
  government commitments and incident response. Each gets a visible subject and
  one working task. Preserve catalogs, effects and causal evidence.
- Title and setup: atlas establishes the campaign; choose the actual nation on it,
  then inspect its strengths and commit to taking office.
- Quarter and ending: editorial reports with causal summaries and complete ledgers;
  the ending's real procedural illustration and epilogue share the opening view.
- News/help/settings: readable secondary reading surfaces, clear close/return and
  consistent controls, with sufficient safe margins in both languages.

## Non-negotiable verification

Retain deterministic game rules, fogged views, saves, localized authored text,
meaningful costs and confirmation. Test portrait fallback, landscape 844×390 and
667×375, EN/RU, safe areas, interruption, all decision families, management actions,
map interactions, full report/ending navigation and real rendered composition.
Screenshots must demonstrate the map and each page's hierarchy; compilation or
geometry alone cannot approve the design. Native claims require native evidence.
